About the Project

This project is an interactive cooking game designed to teach users how to make orange chicken in a fun, engaging, and personalized way. Unlike traditional recipes that require users to follow fixed steps, this application allows individuals to actively participate in the cooking process through a game-based experience.

Users can explore each step of preparing orange chicken, from selecting ingredients to cooking techniques, while making choices that affect the final outcome. The game encourages creativity by allowing users to adjust flavors such as sweetness, spice level, and sauce intensity according to their personal preferences. This makes the learning experience both educational and enjoyable.

The goal of this project is to make cooking more accessible, especially for beginners, by turning it into an interactive and decision-based activity. By combining elements of gaming with practical cooking knowledge, users can gain confidence in the kitchen while having fun experimenting with recipes.

Key Features

  • Interactive step-by-step cooking simulation
  • Customizable ingredients and flavor options
  • Game-based learning for better engagement
  • Beginner-friendly design and instructions
  • Encourages creativity and experimentation
